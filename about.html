About Our Background

Pramuka Wivasayakathwa Development Cooperative Bank is a community-focused financial institution committed to supporting economic growth in the Sabaragamuwa Province.

Who We Are

Pramuka Wivasayakathwa Development Cooperative Bank is a trusted community-based financial institution serving the Sabaragamuwa Province of Sri Lanka. The bank is officially registered and authorized by the Provincial Cooperative Department, ensuring compliance with regulations for money handling and loan issuance. We specialize in Customer savings, Fixed deposits, Personal loans, Business loans, and Vehicle loans, and we are committed to promoting financial stability, economic growth, and sustainable development within our communities.

Our Mission

"To drive sustainable development within our cooperative region by empowering individuals and business communities. We strive to enhance their capabilities through access to technical knowledge, financial resources, and market opportunities, thereby promoting economic, social, and institutional growth and fostering an independent, self-reliant society."

Our Vision

“To promote sustainable development through the establishment of a resilient and inclusive community.”

Our History

Established in 2020 as a regional financial cooperative society dedicated to advancing the economic development of the Sabaragamuwa Province. Since its inception, the bank has focused on providing comprehensive financial facilities to Entrepreneurs and Small-scale businesses, fostering innovation and sustainable growth within the region. Guided by cooperative principles and a commitment to excellence, the bank continues to play a pivotal role in strengthening the local economy and empowering communities through reliable and inclusive financial services.

Over the years, we have supported small businesses, farmers, and families by offering savings, loans, and development programs that strengthen regional economic growth.
